## Why (Olson / Locke)

- Problem is the executive: king → tyrant.
- **Stationary bandit** (`bandits.png`): settled predator has incentive to protect production to tax it; still predation unless constrained.
- Not a revolution *against* earned wealth — protection *of* earned wealth against upper-class plunder.
- Visual bargain path: `bandit_contract.png`, `social_contract2.png`.
- Locke (`locke.jpg`): legitimate government secures life, liberty, property — must not be the chief bandit.

No Douglass North by name; save for Fed lecture. Light groundwork only: secure property → investment → real wealth.

## What

- Rome: overthrow of kings.
- Britain/Europe: parliament evolves from barons outward.
- Top-down sequence: elites constrain king first; towns; then broader inclusion.

## Madison

- `madison_democracy.jfif` — pure democracy vs republic.
- **Critical spoken note:** For Madison, "democracy" meant something close to mob rule / direct assembly. He did **not** oppose election of representatives. Modern "liberal democracy" ≈ his republic.
- Fed 10 one-liner: representation + larger sphere filter faction.

---

## Two sides of the same coin

Republic and democracy converge **when** minority rights — including property — are protected. That was the original point against the stationary bandit *and* against majority plunder.
