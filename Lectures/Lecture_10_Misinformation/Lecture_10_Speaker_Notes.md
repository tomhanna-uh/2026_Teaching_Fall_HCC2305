## Definitions (spoken thin)

- **Information consumers:** us — voters, sharers, citizens who act on claims.
- **Misinformation:** false/misleading, shared *unknowingly*.
- **Disinformation:** false/misleading, *purposely* created and pushed.
- "Lies, damned lies, and statistics" — set up the budget examples.

## Left-coded: defense budget

Keep the full treatment from the older lecture.

- Viral "55% defense" is on the **discretionary** slice (~27% of federal budget), not the total.
- Arithmetic: 55% × ~27% ≈ **~15% of total federal**.
- With state/local, defense share of all government spending is closer to **~8–10%**.
- Same pattern in tax-dollar pie memes with no footnote.
- **Why people who want less defense spending should still care** (Human Economics point): if the underlying policy goal is sound, massively misleading numbers still destroy credibility and distract from real trade-offs (education efficiency, healthcare levels already high federally, etc.).
- Practical + moral costs of spreading bad numbers.

Healthcare / Social Security order of magnitude: honest federal picture shows seniors and healthcare larger than defense — use that to reinforce "argue with real numbers."

Assets: `55percent_defense.jpg`, `tax_dollars_meme.jpg`, `defense_healthcare_coronavirus_meme.jpg` (and any others already in assets).

## Right-coded: AOC meme

- `aoc_guns_meme.png` — fabricated quote about guns not being a right because they would be in the Constitution.
- Debunked (Snopes / FactCheck / PolitiFact). Second Amendment exists; she has said she is a believer in it while supporting restrictions.
- Pattern: invent the quote that makes the opponent look stupid. Same disease, other team.

---

## Right-coded: trade

- `china_pays_tariffs_trump.png` — claim that China pays the tariffs. False: U.S. importers pay duties to CBP; costs largely passed to U.S. buyers.
- `trade_deficit_tp.png` (Cato "toilet paper surplus") — a money trade deficit is a goods surplus. We received the stuff. Not the same as a firm "losing money" with nothing in return.
- Parallel to defense memes: real policy debate + false/confused framing = worse public argument.

Skip immigration this day.

## So what / limiting government

Bad information fuels permanent crisis and reckless uses of coercive power, or blocks reform with noise. Connect to the course theme: we limit government partly because humans are not omniscient and media incentives amplify urgency.

## How to check

- **Ground News** (`ground_news_bias.jpg`): same story, left/center/right breakdown; Blindspots. Link: ground.news. Phone screenshot is tall — keep width modest (~40–45%).
- Habits: pause; slice vs total; primary source for quotes; notice own-side bias.
- Friends: usually **private** correction; show why it is wrong; avoid public dunk.
