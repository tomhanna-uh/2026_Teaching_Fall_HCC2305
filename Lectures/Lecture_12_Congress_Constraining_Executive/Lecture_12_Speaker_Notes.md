## Tax path (slightly longer)

1. **Magna Carta** — consent to extraordinary levies  
2. **Petition of Right 1628** — no forced loans / non-Parliamentary taxes  
3. **Ship Money** — crown tests the limit  
4. **Bill of Rights 1689** (`english_bill.jfif`) — levying by prerogative without Parliament illegal  
5. **Article I** — tax in Congress; revenue bills in the House; appropriations by law  

Timeline graphic: `tax_timeline.jfif`.

## War power

- Britain 1789: crown still has formal **prerogative** to declare war; Parliament has the purse.
- U.S.: Congress declares war; raise armies (2-year appropriation limit); navy; marque and reprisal.
- `1789_war.jfif`.
- **Defer** War Powers Resolution and modern unilateral-force debates to the later War Powers lecture. Emphasize Founding break.
