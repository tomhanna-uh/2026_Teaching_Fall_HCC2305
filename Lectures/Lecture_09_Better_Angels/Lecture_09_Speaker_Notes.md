## Verbal weave — availability

While charts are up: vivid recent events fill the news; gradual improvement does not. Students over-weight what is available in memory. That is why permanent crisis *feels* true even when rates fall.

On each major chart you can ask lightly: “How urgent does this problem feel compared with the trend?”

---

## Discussion bridge (four threads)

1. Government as organized coercive force  
2. Ethics of the use of force  
3. Classical liberal challenge: live together peacefully as dignified equals and empower the individual  
4. Overstatement of problems amid incredible human progress  

Open discussion; do not force a single conclusion.
