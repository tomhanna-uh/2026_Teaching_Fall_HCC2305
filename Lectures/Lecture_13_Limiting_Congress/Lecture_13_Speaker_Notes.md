## Why limit Congress

- Not only kings: legislative majority can still tyrannize.
- Aristotle (`gemini_aristotle_2.jfif`): democracy as many ruling for themselves.
- Madison (`madison_democracy.jfif`): majority faction.
- Crisis + misinformation → overreact relative to long-run progress (`life_expectancy.png` or similar).
- **1789 point (spoken):** people mostly solved their own problems; Congress could still break what worked. (Slide stays clean; you may say the stronger verb in class.)

## Prohibited / Bill of Rights

- §9 categories: criminal-process (habeas, attainder, ex post facto); anti-aristocracy / anti-corruption (titles of nobility, foreign emoluments).
- `congress_shall.jfif` — limits *on Congress*.
- Stress **9th and 10th**.
- Elections: Congress may regulate federal elections, but primary presumption is state-run; states push back on major encroachment.

---

## Structural brakes

- Bicameralism — two houses must agree.
- **Original Senate:** state legislatures appointed Senators (17th changed this).
- Presentment / veto (`checks_arrows.jpg`).
- **Judicial review** — discuss here as hard outer limit; cases later.
- **27th Amendment** — pay changes effective only after next House election.
